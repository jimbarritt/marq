# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-09-26

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Marq has design notes (`doc/agent-harness.md`, `doc/line-number-gutter-arch.md`) and
a mission-tracking plan (`doc/planning/plan.md`), but no fixed home for a decision
that needs its reasoning kept alongside it once made: the alternatives considered,
what was measured, and why the ones not chosen were rejected. `jimbarritt/tsk`
already uses Architecture Decision Records for this.

## Decision

Use Architecture Decision Records, as
[described by Michael Nygard](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions),
numbered sequentially under `doc/adr/`.

## Consequences

See Michael Nygard's article, linked above. `jimbarritt/tsk`'s `docs/adr/` is the
worked example this convention is taken from.
